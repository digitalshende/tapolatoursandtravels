Popular Routes & Destinations

We connect you to the most beautiful hill stations in the Western Ghats. Whether you need a quick weekend escape or a comprehensive tour, we offer daily departures, one-way drops, and round-trip packages for the following popular routes.

⛰️

Pune to Mahabaleshwar

Duration: Approx. 3.5 - 4 Hours

The perfect weekend getaway! Skip the bus crowds and enjoy a scenic, private drive through the beautiful ghats. We offer both one-way drops and round-trip packages. Enjoy a comfortable ride past Wai and Panchgani straight to your hotel.

🌆

Mumbai to Mahabaleshwar

Duration: Approx. 5 - 6 Hours

Escape the hustle of Mumbai for the cool breezes of Mahabaleshwar. Our experienced drivers ensure a smooth, safe journey via the Mumbai-Pune Expressway and the scenic ghat sections. Perfect for family vacations and long weekend trips.

🏞️

Pune/Mumbai to Tapola

The "Mini Kashmir" of Maharashtra

Looking for a peaceful, offbeat destination? Tapola, nestled by the Shivsagar Lake (Koyna backwaters), is a hidden gem. We provide direct cab services to Tapola, perfect for nature lovers, campers, and those looking to enjoy water sports and boat rides.

🏔️

Pune/Mumbai to Panchgani

The Educational & Heritage Hub

Known for its famous boarding schools, Table Land, and strawberry farms, Panchgani is a fantastic destination for families. We offer reliable cab services to Panchgani, ensuring you reach your resort or school campus safely and on time.

🔄

Mahabaleshwar to Pune / Mumbai

Reliable Return Journeys

Ending your vacation? We provide prompt return pickups from Mahabaleshwar to Pune and Mumbai. Our drivers arrive at your hotel on schedule, ensuring you catch your train, flight, or simply get home comfortably without any delays.

Looking for a Custom Route?

Do you want to visit multiple places in one trip? Or need a cab for a completely different destination? We also cover routes to Matheran, Lonavala, Khandala, Alibaug, and Shirdi.
