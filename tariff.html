Tariff & Pricing

At Tapola Tours and Travels, we believe in complete transparency. We don't believe in hidden charges or surprise fees. Our fares are highly competitive and designed to offer you the best value for your money.

Our Pricing Policy

Because every journey is unique, our pricing is tailored to your specific needs. The final fare depends on a few key factors:

  • Vehicle Type: Choose from budget-friendly Sedans, spacious Innova Crystas, or large Tempo Travellers.
  • Travel Route & Distance: Whether you need a one-way drop or a multi-day round-trip, the distance determines the base fare.
  • Seasonality: Rates during peak tourist seasons (like Diwali, Christmas, and summer vacations) may vary slightly due to high demand.
  • Tolls & State Taxes: Applicable interstate permits and toll taxes are calculated based on the current government rates.

What Our Fare Covers

When you book with us, you get a comprehensive, all-inclusive quote. Depending on your package, your fare typically covers:

  • Fuel Charges: Complete fuel costs for the entire journey.
  • Driver Allowance: Fair compensation for the driver's time and meals.
  • Tolls & Parking: Standard toll plaza fees and hotel parking charges (as per itinerary).
  • Vehicle Maintenance: We handle all wear and tear so you don't have to worry.

*Note: Any extra kilometers or additional sightseeing outside the agreed itinerary will be billed separately at a nominal rate, which will be clearly explained to you beforehand.
