Our Well-Maintained Fleet

Your comfort and safety on the road are our top priorities. We take pride in offering a modern, hygienic, and highly maintained fleet of vehicles. Whether you are traveling solo, with family, or in a large group, we have the perfect ride for you.

🚙

Toyota Innova Crysta

The Gold Standard for Outstation Travel

Spacious, luxurious, and incredibly comfortable, the Innova Crysta is our most popular choice for long journeys. It is perfect for families, small groups, and corporate clients who want to travel in absolute comfort, especially on the winding ghat roads to Mahabaleshwar and Tapola.

  • Available in 6+1 and 7+1 seating options.
  • Plush captain seats & excellent suspension for a smooth ride.
  • Ample boot space for all your luggage.
  • Reliable dual AC and top-tier safety features.
🚗

Swift Dzire / Sedan

The Smart & Budget-Friendly Choice

Ideal for couples, solo travelers, and small families, our premium sedans offer a perfect blend of economy and comfort. If you are looking for quick airport transfers or a budget-friendly weekend getaway to the hills, our sedans are agile, fuel-efficient, and very comfortable.

  • Best-in-class mileage (translates to affordable fares for you).
  • Compact enough to easily navigate city traffic.
  • Comfortable seating for up to 3 passengers + 1 driver.
  • Spacious boot for standard luggage.
🚌

Tempo Traveller

Perfect for Large Groups & Corporate Events

Traveling with a big family or planning a corporate team outing? Our Tempo Travellers are designed to keep the whole group together without compromising on comfort. Think of it as a "home away from home" on wheels, perfect for long road trips.

  • High roof for easy standing and moving around.
  • Pushback seats for a relaxing, fatigue-free journey.
  • Huge luggage space for large bags and equipment.
  • Available with AC and Non-AC options.

Our Fleet Promise

Every vehicle in our fleet undergoes regular maintenance and deep cleaning before every trip. We ensure our cars are breakdown-free, sanitized, and equipped with all necessary safety and emergency tools.
